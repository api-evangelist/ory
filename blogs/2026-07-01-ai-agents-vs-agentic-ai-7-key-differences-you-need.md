AI agents handle single tasks, while agentic AI orchestrates many agents into multi-step workflows. The post explains that an AI agent is autonomous software performing one specific function, whereas agentic AI is the coordination layer deploying multiple agents to tackle complex objectives no single agent could achieve alone.
